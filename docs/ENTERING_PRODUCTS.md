# Entering Products

A Product is a specific thing you buy. Enter its pack size and nutrition exactly as printed on
the label: the app works out the rest.

## Pack size

**Pack size** and **Pack unit** are how much the pack contains, in whatever unit fits:

| Product | Pack size | Pack unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tesco Chicken Breast 650g | 650 | g |
| Tesco Whole Milk 1L | 1 | l |
| Stonebaked Pizza | 1 | item |
| Free Range Eggs, 6 | 6 | item |

Leave it blank if you don't know it. Nothing else on the form needs it.

## Servings per pack

Optional. It's how many equal servings the whole pack yields, not how many items you bought.
Those are usually different things:

| Product | Pack size | Servings per pack | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 600g pack of 6 burger buns | 600 g | 6 | each bun is one serving |
| Stonebaked Pizza | 1 item | 4 | cut into quarters |
| 500ml sauce bottle | 500 ml | 10 | 2 tbsp per serving |
| Free Range Eggs, 6 | 6 item | *(leave blank)* | "per item" already means per egg |

Leave it blank whenever the pack size already says what you need. A box of 6 eggs doesn't need
telling that it has 6 servings, because "per item" already means per egg.

## Nutrition

Type the numbers straight off the label. The "per" line above them is worked out for you:

- **No servings recorded**: per 100 g or per 100 ml, matching what every label prints, or
  per item for a pack sold by count (eggs, a single pizza with no serving count).
- **Servings recorded**: per serving, calculated from the pack size and the serving count.
  There's nothing to type for it.

If the label genuinely states something else, such as a bulk pack still quoted "per 100g", or a
serving size with no formal serving count like "per 2 tbsp", use the "use a different
amount"/"use a different unit" link to enter it exactly as printed.
